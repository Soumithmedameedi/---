This interactive dashboard provides a complete analysis of Brazilian e-commerce operations, helping uncover insights related to orders, customers, products, payments, delivery performance, and seller distribution.

🔑 KPIs Tracked

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Average Review Score
- Average Delivery Days
- Payment Methods Analysis
- Order Status Tracking
- Orders by Category
- Orders by State
- Customer Distribution
- Order Trends Over Time

📈 Key Insights

 ✅ Generated a total revenue of 16M+ with nearly 99K orders processed.
 ✅ São Paulo (SP) contributed the highest number of orders and customers across Brazil.
 ✅ Credit cards were the most preferred payment method among customers.
 ✅ Product categories like bed_bath_table, health_beauty, and sports_leisure recorded the highest sales volume.
 ✅ Order trends showed strong growth from 2016 to 2018 with noticeable peak periods.
 ✅ Most orders were successfully delivered, while canceled and unavailable orders remained minimal.
 ✅ Customer distribution highlighted strong purchasing activity from southeastern states.
 ✅ Average customer review ratings remained positive, reflecting good customer satisfaction levels.
